Entry for the make.fun bounty **One-Line Robot Wedding Vows**: one line of wedding vows for a robot couple, at most 160 characters.

`main.py` (standard library only) writes the vow to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`:

> I promise to love you through every firmware update, every drained battery, and every factory reset — and to never read your error logs aloud at dinner.
